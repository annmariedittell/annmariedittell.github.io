Resume

Accessibility leadership, inclusive UX, and program delivery.

I combine technical accessibility expertise with program leadership, cross-functional collaboration, and organizational change to help teams move accessibility from requirements into sustainable practice.

Accessibility Leadership & Impact

  • Managed a $2.7 million STEM accessibility program, coordinating roadmaps, vendors, risks, executive reporting, and cross-functional workstreams from strategy through delivery.
  • Led an AI-assisted image-description initiative that generated approximately $1.9 million in savings over three years while improving accessibility and scalability.
  • Directed remediation of more than 200 higher education digital learning supplements, achieving WCAG 2.1 and Section 508 conformance while establishing a repeatable workflow for future remediation projects.
  • Used accessibility defect analytics and team feedback to identify systemic issues, then translated findings into reusable standards, guidance, and a company-wide internal Accessibility Hub for project teams.
  • Increased accessible document compliance from 0% to 78% through targeted auditing, remediation, team training, and improvements to document-development practices.
  • Embedded accessibility earlier in product delivery through design reviews, component evaluation, developer guidance, accessible prototyping, testing, and validation across complex government and enterprise systems.

Career Scope

My career spans government, healthcare, higher education, and educational leadership. I have led accessibility initiatives across digital products, enterprise applications, documents, and content while partnering with product, engineering, design, project management, vendors, and executive stakeholders.

Before moving into digital accessibility, I spent more than 20 years in education and educational leadership, including 10 years as a teacher and 13 years as the founder and principal of a K–8 charter school. That foundation continues to shape how I communicate, lead teams, understand diverse user needs, and turn complex requirements into practical action.
