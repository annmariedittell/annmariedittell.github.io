Program Management Case Study

Leading Accessibility at Scale: Strategy, Governance, and Delivery

Coordinating budgets, roadmaps, cross-functional workstreams, and delivery across digital learning products.

My Role
Accessibility and Compliance Manager
Budget
$2.7M STEM accessibility
Scope
Digital learning products and content
Focus
Program strategy and delivery

Program Context

Accessible digital learning requires more than reviewing individual products or fixing isolated defects. Across learning products and content, delivery involves coordinating multiple teams, priorities, resources, and accessibility requirements.

As Accessibility and Compliance Manager, I led accessibility programs spanning digital learning products and content, with responsibility for a $2.7 million STEM accessibility budget.

Program Leadership

My role connected accessibility strategy with the ongoing work of product, technology, design, and content teams. Responsibilities included:

  • Managing accessibility roadmaps and cross-functional workstreams
  • Overseeing the STEM accessibility budget and coordinating vendors
  • Managing risks and communicating program progress through executive reporting
  • Aligning stakeholders around accessibility requirements and delivery priorities

Making Accessibility Part of Delivery

I partnered with product and technology teams to improve accessible authoring tools, content creation, and upstream workflows. I also delivered accessibility training and coaching to help teams apply requirements more consistently as work progressed.

Alongside program oversight, I directed remediation of more than 200 digital textbook supplements, achieving WCAG 2.1 and Section 508 conformance while improving upstream workflows.

Innovation and Results

Another initiative I led during this period used AI-assisted image descriptions to improve accessibility and scalability. It generated approximately $1.9 million in savings over three years.

These examples illustrate two connected aspects of program leadership: coordinating ongoing delivery across teams and improving the processes used to deliver accessible learning content at scale.

Key takeaway: Program management creates the conditions for sustained accessibility: clear priorities, coordinated workstreams, responsible resource management, and processes that help teams deliver inclusive products.
