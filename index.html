Program Leadership

Connecting People, Priorities, and Progress.

I lead programs by aligning strategy, people, resources, and delivery. Across digital learning and education, I have managed budgets, coordinated cross-functional workstreams, addressed risks, and improved processes that help teams deliver accessible outcomes at scale.

Program Strategy & Governance

At Pearson, led accessibility programs spanning digital learning products and content. Managed program roadmaps, cross-functional workstreams, vendors, risks, executive reporting, and a $2.7 million STEM accessibility budget, aligning product, technology, design, and content teams around shared goals.

Scaling Delivery & Improving Operations

Directed remediation of more than 200 digital textbook supplements while improving upstream workflows. Led an AI-assisted image-description initiative that generated approximately $1.9 million in savings over three years, improving accessibility and making content production more scalable.

Organizational & Stakeholder Leadership

Founded and operated a K–8 charter school, serving as principal for 13 years with responsibility for strategy, staff, budgets, compliance, projects, and stakeholder relationships. At the Arizona Department of Education, managed projects, communications, training, and statewide implementation with local education agencies.
